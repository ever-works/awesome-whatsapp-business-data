## Overview

Landbot is a no-code chatbot builder that enables businesses to create conversational experiences on WhatsApp, web, and Messenger without coding.

## Features

- No-code chatbot builder
- WhatsApp chatbot creation
- Conversational flow builder
- Lead generation and qualification
- Customer support automation
- Multi-channel deployment (WhatsApp, web, Messenger)
- Integrations with CRM and business tools

## Pricing

Freemium model with a free plan and paid tiers for advanced features.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).

## Integrations

CRM, helpdesk, and marketing platforms via native integrations and API.
