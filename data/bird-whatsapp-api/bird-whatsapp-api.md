## Overview

Bird is a WhatsApp Business API provider (BSP) offering a unified platform for WhatsApp messaging, automation, and customer engagement.

## Features

- **WhatsApp API**: Send notifications, media, and interactive messages from your backend with webhooks for replies and read receipts.
- **Templates**: Design media cards, carousels, and authentication templates in Studio; submit multi-language versions for Meta approval.
- **Conversations**: Reply with free-form text and media during the 24-hour customer service window, with read receipts, typing indicators, and reactions.
- **Native Flows**: Let customers book appointments, pick delivery slots, and submit multi-step forms inside the chat without opening a browser.
- **Broadcasts**: Launch targeted promotions, product drops, and back-in-stock campaigns to opted-in audiences and track resulting replies.
- **AI agents**: Resolve order inquiries and routine support automatically from live backend data, then hand complex threads to your team.
- **Commerce**: Share product cards and catalog items inside the thread so shoppers can browse options and move straight to checkout.
- **Calling**: Escalate complex support or high-consideration sales chats to a voice call inside WhatsApp.
- **Numbers**: Onboard new WhatsApp Business numbers, migrate existing senders from another provider, and manage display profiles in one workspace.
- **Analytics**: Inspect delivery, read, and failure rates by template or country, and reconcile message volume against Meta billing categories.

## Pricing

Commercial platform; pricing is usage-based and available upon request from Bird.
