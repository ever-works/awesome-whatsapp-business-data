## Overview

Botpress is a conversational AI platform for building chatbots and virtual agents with WhatsApp integration capabilities.

## Features

- Visual chatbot flow builder
- Conversational AI and natural language understanding
- WhatsApp integration and deployment
- Multi-channel support
- Bot analytics and monitoring
- Team collaboration tools
- Open source core (Community Edition)

## Pricing

Free tier available; paid plans for advanced features and enterprise usage.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted and self-hosted (open source).

## Integrations

WhatsApp, Messenger, Slack, Telegram, and other messaging channels via API.
