## Overview

Sinch is a cloud communications platform (CPaaS) that provides WhatsApp Business API access as part of its omnichannel messaging suite.

## Features

- WhatsApp Business API access and management
- Omnichannel messaging (WhatsApp, SMS, voice, email)
- Conversational customer support
- Notification and alert delivery
- Marketing campaign messaging
- Enterprise-grade security and compliance

## Pricing

Usage-based pricing with volume discounts. Contact Sinch for enterprise pricing details.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).

## Integrations

CRM systems, contact center platforms, and business tools via API.
