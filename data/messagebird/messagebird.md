## Overview

MessageBird is an omnichannel communications platform that provides WhatsApp Business API access alongside SMS, voice, and email channels.

## Features

- WhatsApp Business API access
- Omnichannel messaging across WhatsApp, SMS, voice, and email
- Shared team inbox for customer conversations
- Chatbot and automation capabilities
- Contact management and segmentation
- Analytics and reporting

## Pricing

Free tier available; paid plans based on message volume and features.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).

## Integrations

CRM, helpdesk, and e-commerce platforms via API and pre-built connectors.
