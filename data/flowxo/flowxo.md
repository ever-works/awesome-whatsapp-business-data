## Overview

Flow XO is a chatbot and workflow automation platform that supports WhatsApp integration for building automated conversational experiences.

## Features

- Chatbot builder for WhatsApp and other channels
- Workflow automation
- Multi-channel messaging (WhatsApp, web, Messenger, SMS)
- Integration with business applications
- Automated responses and conversations
- Lead capture and qualification

## Pricing

Paid plans with a free trial available.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).

## Integrations

WhatsApp, web chat, Messenger, SMS, and various business tools via native integrations.
