## Overview

Callbell is a WhatsApp communication and customer support platform that provides a shared team inbox for managing customer conversations.

## Features

- Shared team inbox for WhatsApp conversations
- Multi-agent collaboration on customer messages
- WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger support
- Conversation assignment and routing
- Contact management and profiles
- Message templates and quick replies
- Analytics and reporting

## Pricing

Paid plans with a free trial available.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).

## Integrations

WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, CRM, and business tools via API and native integrations.
