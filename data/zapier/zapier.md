## Overview

Zapier is a workflow automation platform that connects WhatsApp with thousands of other applications to automate business processes.

## Features

- WhatsApp integration and automation
- Connect WhatsApp to 5,000+ applications
- Multi-step workflows (Zaps)
- Trigger-based automation
- Data synchronization between WhatsApp and business tools
- No-code automation builder

## Pricing

Freemium model with a free plan and paid tiers for higher task volumes and premium features.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted (SaaS).

## Integrations

WhatsApp, CRMs, helpdesks, marketing tools, spreadsheets, and thousands of other SaaS applications.
